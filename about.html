About CCP

Community College of Philadelphia is a public community college in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. The college offers many programs for students who want to continue their education.

CCP has programs in areas such as business, technology, health, science, and liberal arts. Students can choose a program based on their educational and career goals.

Student Life and Education

CCP provides students with classes and resources that can help them reach their academic goals. Students can take classes in person or through other available learning options.
